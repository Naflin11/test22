FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About Tax Planning?

What is the difference between tax planning and tax preparation?
Tax preparation focuses on preparing and filing tax returns. Tax planning involves reviewing financial decisions in advance to identify eligible opportunities and understand potential tax consequences.
When should I start tax planning?
Tax planning can be useful throughout the year. Starting early provides more time to evaluate options before important deadlines or financial decisions.
Can tax planning help small businesses?
Yes. Business owners may benefit from reviewing estimated taxes, expenses, business structure, retirement planning, and other relevant considerations.
Does tax planning guarantee lower taxes?
No. Potential tax savings depend on your circumstances, eligibility, and applicable tax law. The goal is to identify appropriate opportunities and support informed decisions.
Do you offer virtual consultations?
Yes. Nash Tax & Bookkeeping offers remote-friendly consultations for individuals and businesses. You can contact us or book an available appointment online.
